# The Cherimoya Book

The book teaches cherimoya the way a first language should be learned, starting from what a computer is. Each chapter builds on the ones before it and shows each idea with a small program, pointing out what's state, what's an action and what reaches the outside world.

Chapters are outlines until they're written.

1. [The Machine](01-the-machine.md): a computer is a machine you give instructions to
2. [Two Worlds](02-two-worlds.md): the inside, where everything is deterministic, and the outside, where hardware and the OS can fail
3. [States and Actions](03-states-and-actions.md): the inside world is states and actions, categorized by types
4. [Numbers](04-numbers.md)
5. [Choosing](05-choosing.md)
6. [Repeating](06-repeating.md)
7. [Text](07-text.md)
8. [Many Values](08-many-values.md)
9. [Who Owns What](09-who-owns-what.md)
10. [Checking Your Work](10-checking-your-work.md)

More chapters come as the language grows: collections, types of your own, functions as values, contracts, modules, concurrency, and the outside world in full.
